Introduction

Tic-Tac-Toe, is an abstract board game for two players.
It is played in a 3x3 grid and the goal is to align three same symbols in a row, column or diagonal before the opponent.

Game components

  • A square grid with 3 rows and 3 columns (3x3 cells).
  • Two distinct symbols for the players:
    • Player 1: "X"
    • Player 2: "O"

Preparation

  • The grid must be empty before the game starts.
  • Player 1 starts at the first turn.
    • In case of victory of one player and a revenge match is requested, the defeated player will have the right to starts as first at the new match.
    • In case of drawn and a revenge match is requested, the initial turn will go to the player who did not start the previous game.

Game progress

  • Players take turns placing their symbol in an empty space on the grid.
  • It is forbidden to modify or remove a symbol already placed.
  • The game continues until one of the game-ending conditions occurs.

Victory Conditions

A player wins if he manages to line up three of his symbols in one of the following configurations:
  • Three consecutive symbols in horizontal (e.g. a row entirely occupied by "X" or "O").
  • Three consecutive symbols in vertical (e.g. a column entirely occupied by "X" or "O").
  • Three consecutive symbols in diagonal (e.g. a sequence that crosses the grid diagonally from corner to corner).

Drawn

If all 9 squares of the grid are occupied and no player has lined up three symbols, the game ends in a draw.
